Jagua

Jagua is a natural dye derived from the unripe fruit of the Genipa americana tree, native to the rainforests of Central and South America. It is widely used for temporary body art because its juice reacts with the skin to produce a dark blue-black stain that realistically mimics the look of a permanent tattoo.
