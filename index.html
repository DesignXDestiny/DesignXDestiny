Jagua Body Art

My journey with temporary tattoos began in Las Vegas, working on the Strip and Downtown I opened my own business at markets and pop-up events, continuing to share my work after my move to Oregon in 2023. What I love most is creating custom designs for each person. Clients are welcome to choose from my design collection, but I am thrilled by the opportunity to collaborate about ideas, inspirations, and style—to turn that into a one-of-a-kind jagua piece. Every design is made to reflect the individuality of the person wearing it, because no two experiences are the same.

FrontEnd Development

Front-end development is like an evolved form of poetry or English. It’s like an English class in the way that you develop your ideas like an essay, and that’s literally mostly written in English. It is like poetry because less is more in the same way. Much of the drafting process is me trying to say the same thing with fewer code words. Developing web pages, or anything this way, is when I’m creating something to share with someone. This is an opportunity to create with the audience in mind.

For tech insights, coding capabilities, and / or designs. On this page, there is a collection of one-page sites that I created; you are welcome to see my work.
